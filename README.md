# Personal Finance Tracker

**Project type:** Excel Dashboard / Personal Finance Management
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel
**Reporting period:** July 2026 – June 2027

## Summary
An Excel-based workbook designed to monitor income, expenses, monthly budgets, savings, loans, and financial goals — giving a clear view of cash flow, spending behaviour, savings progress, and budget performance in one dashboard.

## Workbook structure
10 linked sheets: **Dashboard** (KPI cards, category spend, budget comparison, goals), **Income**, **Expenses**, **Budget**, **Savings**, **Loans**, **Goals**, **Monthly Summary**, **Categories** (dropdown lists), and a **README** sheet with usage guidance.

## Key metrics (August 2026)
- **Total income:** ₦1,700,000
- **Total expenses:** ₦674,000
- **Net cash flow:** ₦1,026,000
- **Budget variance:** ₦66,000 under budget (of a ₦740,000 monthly budget)
- **Savings:** ₦300,000 · **Savings rate:** 17.65%
- **YTD (Jul–Aug):** ₦1,314,000 spent vs. ₦1,480,000 budgeted — ₦166,000 under plan

## Excel techniques used
- `SUMIFS` for income/expense totals by month and status
- Calculated fields for net cash flow, budget variance, savings rate, and goal progress
- Dashboard sheet pulling live from detail tracker sheets
- Monthly Summary sheet for month-by-month trend review

## Key insights
- Positive cash flow in both July and August 2026
- August income rose ₦50,000 over July due to higher commission
- Expenses stayed under budget both months — good spending discipline
- Top expense categories: Church Giving (17.8%), Food (15.6%), Rent (14.8%), Family Support (11.9%)
- Emergency Fund goal is active: ₦600,000 saved of a ₦4,440,000 target (13.5% progress)

## Recommendations
- Update the tracker weekly to avoid missing transactions
- Replace sample loan records with real loan balances and payment plans
- Set monthly savings targets for the currently inactive goals (Laptop Upgrade, Training/Certification)
- Monitor Food, Personal Care, Education, and Electricity/Security categories closely — most likely to exceed plan
- Add a yearly trend dashboard across the full financial year
